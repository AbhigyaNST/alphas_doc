_Section: Regions and Universes | page id: getting-started-on-gbrdeu_

# Introduction

- Region GBR (country code of United Kingdom) is created by stocks listed on the United Kingdom exchange.
- Region DEU (country code of Germany) is created by stocks listed on the German exchange.
- [Standard submission tests](https://platform.worldquantbrain.com/learn/documentation/interpret-results/alpha-submission) are applied.

# Tips

- You can first try transferring EUR Alphas to the GBR and DEU regions.

- This might provide some performance improvement, but you may often still need to further optimize them for submission.

- In addition to ideas that work in EUR, you can also try specific ideas or datasets that are only available or have higher coverage in GBR and DEU.

- This can provide diversified signals to improve your combined performance.
- Recommended dataset for DEU:

- trend_momentum_model
- analyst56
- analyst_pred_cons
- pv109
- analyst_factor_signals
- fundamental89
- fundamental93
- stock_vol_index
- institutions25
- option_chart_model

- Recommended dataset for GBR:

- analyst83
- analyst56
- pv109
- other384
- event_sentiment_signals
- institutions25
- option_chart_model
- sentiment33

- For DEU Alphas, you can focus more on optimizing the Sharpe ratio, both on the research universe and the sub-universe.
- You might find GBR Alphas easier to create compared to DEU Alphas, which is expected.

- However, creating submittable DEU Alphas can improve your combined performance even further, so both are worth investing time in for signal research.
